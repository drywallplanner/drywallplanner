# Drywall Planner

**[drywallplanner.com](https://drywallplanner.com)** is a free drywall estimator that works without an account.

Type in a room's length, width and wall height, plus any doors and windows. The planner then fits actual 4x8, 4x9, 4x10 or 4x12 boards onto each wall one at a time, reuses leftover cut pieces where they fit, and adds a 10% spare margin.

It also totals the supplies you'll need:

- **Joint compound** at about 140 lb per 1,000 sq ft (a 4.5 gal box weighs roughly 61.7 lb)
- **Paper tape** at about 370 ft per 1,000 sq ft
- **Screws** at around 1 per sq ft on walls and 1.25 per sq ft on ceilings
- **Cost**, if you enter prices (optional)

Openings come off the area automatically: 21 sq ft for each door, 15 for each window and 112 for each garage door. You can work in feet and inches or in metric. Everything runs in your browser, and you can embed the calculator on your own site.

**Example:** a 14 x 12 ft room with 8 ft walls, one door and two windows comes to 365 sq ft net. That's 13 sheets laid out, so you'd buy 15.
